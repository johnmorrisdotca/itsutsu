Kyuubu キューブ

A turning cube, drawn in CSS 3D. Drag a sticker to turn its layer; drag around the cube to look at it. The wheel over a sticker turns the layer under it, Ctrl with the wheel the layer across it, Shift with the wheel the face. Keys: R L U D F B (Shift for the other way), M E S, x y z, a number first for an inner layer, and the arrows to look around.
